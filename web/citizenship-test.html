The Citizenship Practice Test

i

The civics questions asked at the naturalization interview — all of them, from the government's own published list.

This is the 2025 test: 128 questions, 20 asked at the interview, 12 correct to pass.

Filed your N‑400 before 20 October 2025?

i

A question counts as learned once you have answered it correctly twice, on separate showings. One right answer could be luck, and luck does not survive being asked again. Getting it wrong sets that question back to zero.

Recall
i

Questions come two ways. Multiple choice gives five options with one right answer. Recall asks you to type the answer first, then shows every accepted one — closer to the real interview, where nobody reads you a list.

This sets how often recall comes up. 21 questions are recall whatever you pick, because every acceptable answer is on the list and there is no honest wrong option to put beside it — naming an American Indian tribe, for instance. So Less reduces recall rather than removing it.

What you type is never graded. Too many wordings are right for any checker to judge fairly, and marking a correct answer wrong would be worse than useless. Writing it down first is what makes your own verdict honest — then you say whether you had it.
