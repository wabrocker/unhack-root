The Citizenship Test

The civics questions asked at the naturalization interview — all of them, from the government's own published list.

First: which test applies to you?

There are two civics tests in force, and which one you sit depends on when your Form N-400 was filed. Practising the wrong bank before a real interview is worse than not practising, so this asks before it starts.

You take the 2008 test — 100 questions, 10 asked, 6 correct to pass. This tool currently covers only the 2025 test (128 questions, 20 asked, 12 to pass), so studying here would be the wrong material.

Go to the USCIS 2008 test page instead. The 2008 set is on the list to add here.

Eight questions are not asked here
