The Citizenship Test

The civics test asked at the naturalization interview — the real questions, from the government's own published list.

Which test applies to you In progress

There are currently two civics tests in force, and which one you sit depends on when your Form N-400 was filed:

  • Filed before 20 October 2025 — the 2008 test. Up to 10 questions drawn from a bank of 100; 6 correct to pass.
  • Filed on or after 20 October 2025 — the 2025 test. 20 questions drawn from a bank of 128; 12 correct to pass.

Most study material still shows only the older test, which is the single most consequential thing to get right here: practising the wrong bank before a real interview is worse than not practising at all. This tool will ask your filing date before it asks anything else.

Source: USCIS — 2025 Civics Test. Questions published by USCIS are U.S. Government works.

Why it is here, and not only for applicants

It is a short, concrete, checkable map of how the federal government is actually assembled — and unlike almost everything else in civics, there is an official answer key. Most people born here have never sat it.

If you are preparing for a real interview, treat this as practice and USCIS as the authority. We will always link you back to the source.
