Unhacking Democracy

in the United States

Knowledgeable, cross-partisan citizens teaming up to save Democracy.

i

Our mission is to educate citizens on how democratic systems have been exploited. We provide specific, actionable, cross-partisan ways to help repair them. That work rests on three things: knowledge you can verify, strength in numbers, and clarity about what each of our teams is for.

Browse the shelves, or ask a librarian.

A small collection, chosen on purpose. Every book here teaches something you keep.

i

What this place is for. Democratic systems get exploited in ways that are, on the whole, perfectly legal — and the repair work is ordinary, unglamorous and available to anyone. This is where the tools for it are kept.

Whatever you believe and whoever you vote for: if you want this country’s democracy to work, you are in the right place.

The one rule for what goes on the shelf: does it teach you something you keep? Pledges, campaigns and particular bills may be worth your time — they are just not what this room is for, and we leave them off whoever they come from, ourselves included.
