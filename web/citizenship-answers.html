Your Eight Answers

Eight questions are not in the practice test. Their answers change, or they depend on where you live. This page helps you find all eight and keep them.

Why these eight are different

The other 120 questions have answers that stay the same. These eight do not.

Four of them change when there is an election or a new appointment. Three depend on your state or your district. One — your state capital — never changes, so we can just tell you.

The interview can ask any of them. So it is worth doing this once, writing the answers down, and keeping them.

Your answers stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The eight questions come from USCIS, 2025 civics test. USCIS is the authority. This page only helps you find the answers.
