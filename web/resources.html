Unhacking Democracy

in the United States

Knowledgeable, cross-partisan citizens teaming up to save Democracy.

i

Our mission is to educate citizens on how democratic systems have been exploited. We provide specific, actionable, cross-partisan ways to help repair them. That work rests on three things: knowledge you can verify, strength in numbers, and clarity about what each of our teams is for.

Resources

Find Your Next Step gives you something to do. These are for understanding what you are doing it to — because knowing the shape of the system is the difference between an opinion and a lever.

Start anywhere. If you are not sure, the practice test below is the quickest way to find out what you already know and what you only think you know.
