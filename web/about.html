Unhacking Democracy

in the United States

Knowledgeable, cross-partisan citizens teaming up to save Democracy.

i

Our mission is to educate citizens on how democratic systems have been exploited — and to provide specific, actionable, and cross-partisan ways to help repair them using the “tri-pronged” power of verifiable knowledge, strength in numbers and clarity of all of our teams’ purposes.

Any system of rules can be subverted by those who find and exploit its loopholes — that's what a hack is, in a legislature as much as in a computer. The powerful hack best because they can afford the lawyers, lobbyists, and engineers to find the gaps, and then make the exploit permanent. Unhacking means giving ordinary citizens the same kind of leverage: not a security researcher's skill set, because these exploits happen in public — just the tools to see them and act.

Cross-partisan by architecture

Every tool here weights toward work with genuine support across parties — transparency, election security, anti-corruption — and away from candidate- or party-specific asks.

Every claim links to a primary source

Nothing here asks for your trust on faith. Any AI assistance summarizes and cites; it never editorializes, and you can always check the source yourself.
