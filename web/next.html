Unhacking Democracy

in the United States

Knowledgeable, cross-partisan citizens teaming up to save Democracy.

i

Our mission is to educate citizens on how democratic systems have been exploited. We provide specific, actionable, cross-partisan ways to help repair them. That work rests on three things: knowledge you can verify, strength in numbers, and clarity about what each of our teams is for.

You can’t choose because you’re not supposed to be able to.

The feed that shows you the problems is built to hold your attention, it never ends, and it stops just short of anything you could actually do. The overwhelm is its output. It isn’t your failing.

So this page does one thing: it asks you five questions and gives you one next step. Not a list. One.

What this does and doesn’t do

  • Nothing you type leaves this browser. No account, no sign-up, no analytics on your answers, nothing stored on a server. Reload the page and it is all gone.
  • The resources aren’t ours. They come from Ordinary People, Extraordinary Times. We didn’t write them and we don’t host them — we sorted them and decided which one to hand you.
  • We left things off that shelf. Anything that recruits you for a position rather than teaching you something you keep. A campaign push expires in November; knowing how to check a claim doesn’t.
  • It doesn’t know where you live yet. Most civic work is local and this first version is not, which is a real limit rather than an oversight.
  • It doesn’t read what you typed. The last question is for you, not for us — naming the thing that brought you is worth doing, but this version doesn’t route on it.
