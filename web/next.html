Unhacking Democracy

in the United States

Knowledgeable, cross-partisan citizens teaming up to save Democracy.

i

Our mission is to educate citizens on how democratic systems have been exploited. We provide specific, actionable, cross-partisan ways to help repair them. That work rests on three things: knowledge you can verify, strength in numbers, and clarity about what each of our teams is for.

One thing at a time.

You can’t fix everything. Nobody can. But you can take one positive step — and then another. And join the growing team effort to get Democracy back on track.

Answer five quick questions. We’ll pick one action that matches the time you’ve got and the kind of person you are. Do it, come back, and the next one is ready.

Whatever you believe and whoever you vote for — if you want this country’s democracy to work, you are in the right place.

i
  • Nothing you type leaves this browser. No account, no sign-up, no analytics on your answers, nothing stored on a server. Reload the page and it is all gone.
  • Everything here teaches you something you keep. We left off anything that was really about signing you up for a position. A campaign push expires in November; knowing how to check a claim doesn’t.
  • The guides aren’t ours. They come from Ordinary People, Extraordinary Times. We didn’t write them and we don’t host them — we sorted them and decided which one to hand you.
  • That sorting is our opinion. We kept the ones that teach you something you keep, and left off policy asks, pledges and campaign drives — from anyone, ourselves included. That’s about what this page is for, not about whether those things are worth doing. What we left off, and why, is written down in public.
  • It doesn’t know where you live yet. Most civic work is local and this first version isn’t, which is a real limit rather than an oversight. It is the next thing we want to fix.
  • It doesn’t read what you typed. The last question is for you, not for us — naming the thing that brought you is worth doing on its own, but this version doesn’t pick based on it.
  • “Finished it” is your own count. Nobody checks and nobody is told. It is a tally you keep for yourself, in this browser.
